# Road Trip — Rules (v1)

## Goal

First to exactly **1000** miles wins. Cannot overshoot. Max **two** 200-mile cards per hand.

## Turn

1. Draw one card (automatic at turn start).
2. Play one card **or** discard one.
3. Hand size returns to 6 (except mid-safety extra turns).

## Drive

Miles require **Drive** on top of your battle pile, unless **Emergency Vehicle** is active and you are not blocked by a non-traffic hazard.

## Hazards

- **Red Light, Accident, Out of Gas, Flat Tire, Traffic Jam, GPS Error** — battle pile (play onto opponent's Drive).
- **Speed Limit** — speed pile; while active, only 25/50 mile cards.

## Remedies

Match the hazard. After flat/gas/accident/jam/gps remedy, play **Drive** before miles (unless Emergency Vehicle). Red Light is cleared by Drive. Speed Limit cleared by End of Limit.

## Safeties

Permanent immunity. Playing a safety grants an **extra turn**. Matching safety clears a showing hazard.

| Hazard | Remedy | Safety |
|---|---|---|
| Red Light | Drive | Emergency Vehicle |
| Accident | Repairs | Driving Ace |
| Out of Gas | Gasoline | Extra Tank |
| Flat Tire | Spare Tire | Puncture-Proof |
| Speed Limit | End of Limit | Emergency Vehicle |
| Traffic Jam | Traffic Clear | Fast Lane |
| GPS Error | Navigation Fix | GPS Lock |

## Coup Fourré

When hit by a hazard, if you hold the matching safety, play it immediately, discard the hazard, skip intervening players, then take your normal turn.
